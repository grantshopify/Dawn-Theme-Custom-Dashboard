Shipment card

Ten ways to show where an order is.

Each concept uses only what Shopify returns for a shipment: the status, the shipped date, an estimated delivery date when the carrier gives one, and the carrier and tracking number. Switch the status below to watch every card animate. Sample data only.

Status
01

Ruler

A measured scale with a sliding marker. Calm and precise; fits the editorial Wordmark look.

Try: click a stage name under the ruler.
02

Countdown

One giant number (days to go) and five blocks that fill in sequence. The current block moves.

Try: hover a block.
03

Route

A curved route from the warehouse city to the delivery city; the line draws itself and a marker travels along it.

Try: switch status to watch the marker travel.
04

Log

A vertical, Shopify-style timeline. The current step is open; any step expands for details.

Try: click any step.
05

Dial

A progress ring with the step in the middle and the stage list beside it.

Try: switch status, the ring sweeps.
06

Segments

A segmented control: each stage is a tab with its own details panel.

Try: click each segment.
07

Ticket

A boarding-pass layout: from/to, carrier, tracking number on a tear-off stub, and a status stamp.

Try: copy the tracking number, set Delivered for the stamp.
08

Dark band

Matches the Overview’s dark band: a huge status word that rises in, a thin progress line, details in a drawer.

Try: “Shipment details”.
09

Map-led

An abstract city map (no Google until asked) with a pin moving from warehouse to home.

Try: switch status, the pin moves.
10

Stage cards

Swipeable cards, one per stage, snapping to the current one. Good on phones.

Try: swipe or use the arrows.
